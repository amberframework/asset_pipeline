Resolution of the three TIMEOUT rows (buttons, dialogs, selection-controls)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
They do not hang and they do not reach the renderer. They sit in a silent
restart loop: libandroid_material_host.so was observed loading 61 times in one
cleared logcat window while the pid kept changing, with ZERO "Fatal signal"
lines and zero exception text.

Cause: an uncaught Crystal exception in a --shared library unwinds to
__crystal_raise -> exit(). exit() ends the process cleanly, so there is no
signal, no tombstone, and no Java stack trace; Crystal's own message goes to
stderr, which Android does not route to logcat. Android then relaunches the
activity, and the cycle repeats.

The same defect is visible in its louder form on the six CRASH rows, where
exit() runs __cxa_finalize -> art::Mutex::~Mutex -> abort() and the process
dies with SIGABRT instead. Same root cause
(UI::DesignTokens::AndroidRendererNotImplemented raised from
UI::Android::Renderer#theme_color_to_argb), two different exit paths.

Honest tally: 4 of 13 studies render. The other 9 all fail on the same
sentinel-colour limitation; 6 were caught red-handed with the exception name
in a tombstone, 3 exited too quietly to leave one.

Consequence worth flagging: because the process dies at the OS level rather
than raising, MainActivity's "Renderer mount pending native attach" fallback
is unreachable in practice. runCatching cannot catch exit() or SIGABRT.
