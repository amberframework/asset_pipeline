# Cards

- Study purpose: validate elevated, outlined, and tonal card defaults driven by shared tokens on Android.
- Native classes involved: `com.google.android.material.card.MaterialCardView`, `android.widget.LinearLayout`, `android.widget.TextView`.
- Current renderer status: accepted after current phone, tablet, light, and dark captures from April 18, 2026.
- Light appearance notes: filled, outlined, and elevated surfaces render consistently with shared tokens and no placeholder chrome.
- Dark appearance notes: surface separation, outline treatment, and supporting containers remain stable in dark mode.
- Phone and tablet observations: both device classes capture cleanly without visible adaptive regressions.
- Open deviations and promotion impact: no current renderer fidelity blocker remains for the phase-0 Android Material acceptance bar.
